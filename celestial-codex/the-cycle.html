The long line is the year, broken into twelve spans — one for each frame of the Codex. The gold marker is today. The bright segment is the season the sun is moving through right now; tap any segment to read what that period brings. The short line below is the moon’s 29½-day loop; tap any dot to learn the phase and its use.
